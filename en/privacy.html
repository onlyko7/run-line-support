PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Run Line processes only the information needed to manage running challenges and takes the protection of health data seriously.

Effective date: November 7, 2026

1. Information and purposes

Run Line processes challenge names, start and destination coordinates, challenge status, running distances, and dates entered by you to provide challenge features and calculate progress. This information is stored on your device for app functionality.

If you choose a start date for Apple Health integration in a challenge and grant permission, the app reads running workouts and their associated distance from that date onward and adds them to the challenge records. Health and workout information read from HealthKit is not used for advertising or marketing, shared with third parties, or sent to Run Line servers.

The app may request access to your device’s current location to set the initial map view when creating a challenge. Your current location is used only to display the map initially; it is not stored as challenge data or sent to Run Line servers.

2. Storage and retention

Challenges and distance records are stored on your device. Information stored in the app may be deleted if you reset data in the app settings or delete the app. Run Line does not create, modify, or delete the original data in Apple Health.

3. Third-party services

The app may use Google Maps SDK for iOS to display maps. Information processed by Google while providing map services may be subject to Google’s privacy policy. For details, see Google Privacy Policy.

4. Your choices and rights

You can change location and Apple Health permissions in your device settings. You can still use features that do not require those permissions, such as manually entering distance. You can delete local challenges, distance records, and settings using the in-app data reset feature.

5. Children’s privacy

Please refer to the app information on the App Store for Run Line’s intended audience and age rating. The operator protects children’s personal information in accordance with applicable laws.

6. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when app features or applicable laws change. Updates and their effective dates will be posted on this page. You can send privacy-related inquiries to the email below.
