QIs the straight line on the map my actual running route?
No. It visualizes progress between your challenge’s start and destination. It does not show roads, a GPS route, or your actual location while running.
QCan I enter a distance manually?
Yes. Enter a distance and date after your run. Manual entry is available whether or not you connect Apple Health.
QWhat if an Apple Health workout overlaps with a manual entry?
Health records and manual entries on the same date are shown separately and normally both count toward your total. If they are the same run, swipe left on the manual entry marked as a possible duplicate and delete it. You’ll be asked to confirm, and you can undo the deletion for a short time.
QWhere are challenges and records stored?
Challenges and distance records are stored on the device where the app is installed. Deleting the app or resetting its data may remove local records.
QCan I edit or delete a record?
In an active challenge, you can edit or delete records entered manually. Records imported from Apple Health cannot be edited or deleted in Run Line. Records in completed challenges are view-only.
QCan I change a completed challenge?
Completed challenges and their records are view-only. Deleting a challenge from completed history also deletes its associated records.
QDoes resetting data also affect Apple Health?
No. Resetting data removes Run Line challenges, distance records, and settings from this device only. It does not change original workout data in Apple Health.
QHow do I change the app language?
Run Line does not have an in-app language menu. On your iPhone, open Settings > Apps > Run Line > Language, then choose Korean or Japanese. The language selector on this support site changes only the website. The setting name or location may vary by iOS version.
